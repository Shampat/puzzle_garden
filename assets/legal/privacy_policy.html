Privacy Policy - Puzzle Garden

Last updated: Oct 14, 2026

Puzzle Garden (com.vasapps.puzzle) does not collect personal data. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads.

Ads

AdMob may collect Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) to show personalized ads. You can reset this in Android Settings. For EU/EEA/UK users, consent is requested via Google UMP.

Permissions

We only request INTERNET permission to load ads. No Camera, Microphone, Location, Storage, Contacts.

Data Safety

No data collected, no data shared except Advertising ID for ads. No encryption needed for local puzzles. Local progress stored on device via SharedPreferences.

Children

Not directed to children under 13. Family-friendly logic puzzles.
